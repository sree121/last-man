      SURVIVOR NOTES

      • Don't count the chant. It's a different length every time, and it can rush or go silent.
      • Watch the head. A twitch is a bluff. It only counts when it fully turns. It may also peek back while the light is still red.
      • Tap, don't hold. Each tap is a stride. Faster taps, faster runner.
      • You slide after your last tap. Stop a beat early, not when you see red.
      • Go on the first note. Moving the instant green starts gives a quick-start boost.
      • Finish fast to earn tap tests for the bridge.
      • Tug of war is rhythm, not mashing. Wait for the green zone. Save LEAN BACK for their HEAVE.
      • In Mingle, you count. Groups of 3 means grab 2 people. Rooms fill fast.
      • Watch the floodlights. Tempered glass throws back a white glint.
      • In the final, bait the charge. Sidestep near the edge and he can fly off the court.

      HOW TO PLAY

      ENTER THE GAME plays all five games in order. You can also jump straight into games 1–4 from the title screen and carry on from there. The Final Duel unlocks once you've beaten any 2 of the other games. Beaten games get a green corner.

      ROUND 1 · RED LIGHT, GREEN LIGHT

      • Tap Space, ↑, the RUN button or the screen to run. Every tap is a stride: tap faster to run faster. Stop tapping and you stop.
      • The Watcher chants with its back turned, then turns around. Every chant is a different length (3 to 12 notes). It can suddenly rush, go silent for a moment without turning, or twitch its head as a bluff.
      • After a red light it sometimes starts turning away, then peeks back. Only move once the light says GREEN.
      • If you're still moving once it's facing you, the wall guns open fire. You need about 0.2s to skid to a stop.
      • The speed meter under the button shows how fast you're going. At top speed you skid for about 0.3s after your last tap, so stop a little earlier.
      • Tap within 0.3s of green light for a quick start: extra speed for a moment.
      • Cross the red line before the clock hits zero. Stopping within 0.12s of the turn is a close call, worth +250.

      ROUND 2 · TUG OF WAR

      • Your team of ten faces another over a drop. Pull the rope past the middle until they fall.
      • Press Space, ↑, PULL or the screen when the white marker is inside the green zone. The bright center is a perfect pull. Pressing outside the zone makes your team slip.
      • When the other team shouts HEAVE!, press ↓ or LEAN BACK within the warning to brace and make them stumble. Bracing too early wastes it for 1.6s.
      • Six or more perfect pulls earns a bonus tap test for the bridge.

      ROUND 3 · MINGLE

      • Ride the carousel while the music plays. When it stops, a group size is called.
      • Move with arrows/WASD or hold the screen where you want to go. Touch free-standing players to add them to your group. You count as one.
      • Walk into an open (green) door with exactly the right number. One group per room, and the doors lock after 13 seconds.
      • Two songs. Every second left on the lock clock when you get in is added to the bridge clock.

      ROUND 4 · THE GLASS BRIDGE

      • Pick a vest from 1 to 16. Players cross in vest order, one at a time.
      • Each step has two panes. One holds you, the other shatters. Panes that held are marked green.
      • Press ←/→ (or tap the left or right half of the screen) to jump.
      • Floodlights flash every few seconds, and tempered panes catch the light.
      • T spends a tap test to crack the weak pane of the next step.
      • Every unknown pane you survive is worth +500. The whole group shares a 90-second clock, plus whatever you earned in Mingle.

      ROUND 5 · THE FINAL DUEL

      • One rival guards the circle at the head of the court. Reach it within 45 seconds to win.
      • Move with arrows/WASD or by holding the screen. Space or DASH lunges forward.
      • When your rival flashes, he's about to charge. Sidestep, then dash into him while he's dizzy to shove him off the court.
      • If you get shoved off the chalk lines, you lose. Guessing 3+ panes on the bridge makes your dash recharge faster.
